About FastLoan

FastLoan exists for the loans banks don't bother with — small, short, and needed now. We lend between ₹1,000 and ₹5,000, repaid over one to four months, to people covering a genuine short-term gap: a bill that landed early, a repair, a bridge until the next payday.

We don't run your file through a scoring model and hand you a rejection. A person on our team looks at what you've told us and decides based on your actual situation. If the amount or the timing doesn't work for you, we'll tell you before you apply, not after.

Every offer we send shows the full cost of the loan — the processing fee, the service charge, and the exact EMI — worked out before you accept anything. No part of the cost is revealed later.

Terms & conditions

The short version: we tell you the full cost upfront, and expect repayment on the dates agreed. The full clauses are below.

You must be at least 18 years old, a resident of India, and hold a valid government ID and an active bank account in your own name. FastLoan may request additional verification before disbursing any amount.

Loans are offered between ₹1,000 and ₹5,000, repayable over a tenure of 1 to 4 months as agreed at the time of disbursal. The final amount and tenure approved may differ from what was requested, based on our team's review.

A one-time processing fee is deducted from the loan amount before disbursal. A service charge, calculated on the loan amount and tenure, is included in the EMI schedule. Both figures are shown in full before you accept an offer, and neither changes after acceptance.

EMIs are due on the dates set out in your offer, typically in equal monthly instalments. Repayment can be made through the method shared by our team at disbursal.

Missed due dates may attract additional charges. Continued non-payment may be referred to recovery and reported as permitted under applicable regulations. If you expect to miss a payment, contact our team beforehand — we'd rather work out a plan than escalate.

You may repay your loan in full before the end of its tenure. Ask our team about any adjustment to the service charge for early closure.

Information you share is used only to assess and service your application, and is not sold or shared beyond what's needed for servicing your loan or meeting a regulatory requirement.

FastLoan may decline any application, or offer a lower amount or a different tenure than requested, without being obligated to give a specific reason.

These terms may be updated from time to time. The version in effect at the time your offer is made applies to that loan for its full tenure.

For any question about your loan or these terms, use the contact details shared with you in your offer communication. We aim to respond within one working day.

This summary is provided for clarity and does not replace the specific terms confirmed in your individual loan offer.
